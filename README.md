# Precious Foods Sales & Profitability Analysis

## 1. Project Overview

This project explores how sales, expenses, and profit can be analyzed to
understand product performance and support business decision-making.

The project uses illustrative sample data for three products: Rice,
Beans, and Oil. The analysis is designed as preparation for a Power BI
dashboard.

**Project status:** README and dashboard plan in progress. The Power BI
dashboard has not yet been built.

## 2. Business Objective

The objective is to understand: - Which product generates the highest
sales. - Which product contributes the most profit. - How expenses
compare with sales for each product. - Which products may need further
investigation to improve profitability.

## 3. Tools and Skills

-   **Planned tool:** Microsoft Power BI
-   Business data analysis
-   Sales and profitability analysis
-   Data interpretation
-   Business recommendations
-   Dashboard planning

## 4. Sample Data

The figures below are illustrative and are used for learning and
practice.

  Product           Sales (₦)   Expenses (₦)    Profit (₦)
  ----------- --------------- -------------- -------------
  Rice                500,000        350,000       150,000
  Beans               300,000        180,000       120,000
  Oil                 200,000        150,000        50,000
  **Total**     **1,000,000**    **680,000**   **320,000**

## 5. Key Findings

-   Total sales are ₦1,000,000.
-   Total profit is ₦320,000.
-   Overall profit margin is 32%.
-   Rice has the highest sales and total profit among the three
    products.
-   Oil has the lowest total profit and the highest expense ratio at
    75%.

## 6. Business Recommendations

-   Investigate Oil's purchasing, transportation, and other operating
    costs.
-   Monitor Rice because it contributes 50% of total sales.
-   Review sales, expenses, and profit together before making
    product-related decisions.
-   Use future actual business data to validate these preliminary
    findings.

## 7. Planned Dashboard

The planned Power BI dashboard will include:

-   KPI cards for total sales, total profit, and profit margin.
-   A bar chart comparing sales by product.
-   A bar chart comparing profit by product.

These visuals are intended to help a business owner quickly understand
product performance and identify areas that may need attention.

## 8. Limitations

This is a practice project based on illustrative data, not verified
client data. The findings and recommendations are preliminary. The Power
BI dashboard has not yet been implemented, so no completed dashboard or
interactive report is being presented.
