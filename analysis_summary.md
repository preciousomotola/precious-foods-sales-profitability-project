# Project 4: Sales & Profitability Analysis

## 1. Overview
This analysis examines sales, expenses, profit, and profit margins for Rice, Beans, and Oil using illustrative sample data.

## 2. Key Results

| Product | Sales (₦) | Expenses (₦) | Profit (₦) | Profit Margin |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Rice | 500,000 | 350,000 | 150,000 | 30% |
| Beans | 300,000 | 180,000 | 120,000 | 40% |
| Oil | 200,000 | 150,000 | 50,000 | 25% |
| **Total** | **1,000,000** | **680,000** | **320,000** | **32%** |

## 3. Key Insights
- **Rice** generated the highest sales and total profit.
- **Beans** had the highest profit margin at 40%.
- **Oil** had the lowest total profit and the highest expense ratio at 75%.
- Total profit was ₦320,000 from ₦1,000,000 in sales.

## 4. Recommendations
1. Review Oil's purchasing and operating costs to identify possible savings.
2. Maintain Rice's sales performance while monitoring expenses.
3. Consider ways to grow Beans sales because of its higher profit margin.
4. Review sales, expenses, and profit regularly to support better decisions.

## 5. Data Note
The figures are illustrative sample data for portfolio practice, not verified results from an actual business.
